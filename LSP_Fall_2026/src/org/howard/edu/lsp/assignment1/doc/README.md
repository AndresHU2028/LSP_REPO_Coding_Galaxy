1) Are late programming assignments accepted?

No, late programming assignments will be accepted.

2) Where will official course announcements be posted?

Official course announcements will be posted on the course Piazza page.

3) How will examination dates be announced?

Currently, examination dates are TBD, yet when a date is picked for the mid-term and final examination, they will be announced first in class and then posted on Piazza at least two weeks in advance.

4) Under what circumstances will a makeup examination be considered?

Makeup examinations are granted only under extraordinary circumstances beyond the student's control. Students requesting a makeup must notify the instructor as soon as possible and provide supporting documentation that verifies the student was unable to participate in the examination. The instructor will evaluate the documentation and circumstances around the absence to determine if a makeup exam is permitted. Note that doctor's note or other documents that verify medical visits do not automatically qualify a student for a makeup exam.

5) Is AI permitted in this course?

AI tools are permitted and can be used only when permitted by the instructor or the specific assignment requirements. Unless otherwise stated, students are responsible for all submitted work.
